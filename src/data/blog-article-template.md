# Blog Article Writing Template

## 🎯 Purpose
Use this template to write original blog articles for MovieVault.
Each article should be 500-800 words of YOUR original content.

---

## Article Structure

### 1. Article Metadata
```typescript
{
  id: "your-article-url-slug",
  title: "Your Compelling Title Here",
  excerpt: "A 2-3 sentence summary that makes people want to read more.",
  author: "MovieVault Editorial",
  date: "2025-11-06",
  category: "Film Analysis", // or Film History, Recommendations, etc.
  readTime: "8 min read",
  image: "/placeholder.svg"
}
```

### 2. Article Content Sections

#### A. Introduction (50-100 words)
- Hook the reader
- State what the article is about
- Why should they care?

#### B. Main Content (400-600 words)
- Break into 3-5 subsections
- Use specific examples
- Share YOUR insights and opinions
- Include anecdotes or personal experiences

#### C. Conclusion (50-100 words)
- Summarize key points
- End with a thought-provoking statement
- Optional: Call to action

## ✅ Quality Checklist

Before publishing your article, check:

- [ ] Is this YOUR original perspective?
- [ ] Have you shared specific examples?
- [ ] Is it 500-800 words?
- [ ] Does it provide genuine value to readers?
- [ ] Have you avoided copying from other sources?
- [ ] Does it sound like a human wrote it? (Not AI)
- [ ] Have you proofread for errors?
- [ ] Is the title compelling?
- [ ] Does the excerpt make people want to read more?

---

## 🚫 What to Avoid

- Don't copy content from other websites
- Don't use ChatGPT to write entire articles
- Don't write generic content that could be anywhere
- Don't forget to add YOUR personal touch
- Don't use clickbait without delivering value

---

## 💡 Writing Tips

1. **Write Like You Talk**: Be conversational
2. **Use Examples**: Specific movies, directors, scenes
3. **Share Opinions**: Don't be afraid to have a perspective
4. **Be Helpful**: Teach readers something
5. **Stay Authentic**: Let your personality shine through

---

## 📊 Recommended Publishing Schedule

**Phase 1 (Before AdSense):**
- Week 1-2: Write 10 articles
- Week 3: Write 5 more articles
- Week 4: Review, edit, publish all 15

**Phase 2 (After AdSense Approval):**
- Add 2-3 new articles per month
- Keep content fresh and relevant
